# ☢️ Nuclear Expansion Pack




A nuclear-powered parts pack for **Spaceflight Simulator (SFS)**. Brings Nuclear Thermal Rocket (NTR) and ion propulsion technology to your deep-space missions.

## 📋 Overview

Conventional engines not cutting it for interstellar travel? This pack adds high-Isp nuclear engines, including KSP-inspired classics and original designs. Lower thrust, far better fuel economy.

## 🚀 Parts List

### Engines

| Part | Thrust (t) | Isp (s) | Best For |
|------|------------|---------|-----------|
| Nuclear Hawk | 150t | 300s | Upper stage / outer space. Nuclear version of Hawk engine. Expandable nozzle – no torque. |
| Nuclear Frontier | 70t | 800s | Interstellar journeys. Nuclear version of Frontier engine. Higher Isp, slightly lower thrust. |
| LV-N "Nerv" | 60t | 800s | Classic KSP nuclear engine. Balanced all-rounder. |
| IX-6315 Dawn | 5t | 4200s | Ion engine for tiny probes. Buffed 25–250x from stock KSP (stock 0.2t/0.02t is unusable). Extremely low thrust, extremely high Isp. |
| Nuclear Valiant | 50t | 500s | Nuclear version of Valiant engine. Mid-range option. |
| Vesta VR1 | 125t | 390s | Small, efficient upper stage motor for probes and satellite launch vehicles. |
